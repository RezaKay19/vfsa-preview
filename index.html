"A parent's love is the lantern that never dims, even when the road is hard to find."

Debbie Jackson has been facilitating grief support groups for the past five years, walking alongside individuals and families impacted by substance use. After losing her son seven years ago to fentanyl poisoning, Debbie felt called to help others navigating the same painful journey. Her mission is simple but powerful: no one should have to grieve this kind of loss alone.
